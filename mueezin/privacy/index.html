Mueezin — Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026 · Updated 31 October 2026 · Published by ANA Finance, Dubai (United Arab Emirates)

Mueezin does not collect or store your location or any other data. No account, no ads, no trackers, no server of ours. Prayer times, qibla and your settings are calculated and kept on your iPhone. One feature uses Apple Maps: when you search for nearby mosques, the map search area is sent to Apple to return results, under Apple's privacy policy (see "Nearby mosques" below). Nothing is sent to ANA Finance.

What the app does

Mueezin is a free iPhone app: prayer times calculated on the device, notifications, qibla direction and a tasbih counter. It works without an Internet connection, except the Nearby mosques search, which needs one.

Location

If you allow it ("While Using the App"), the app reads your iPhone's location requesting about one-kilometre accuracy. It uses it only to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction, on the device. ANA Finance does not receive your location: the app has no server of ours to send it to. The only exception is the Nearby mosques search described below, which sends a map search area to Apple. You can also refuse location and choose a city in the app. The last known location is kept on your iPhone so that times show at the next launch without a network; choose a city or delete the app to remove it.

Nearby mosques

Mueezin does not collect or store your location. When you search for nearby mosques (Nearby mosques screen, in versions that include it, and only if you allowed location), the map search area is sent to Apple Maps to return results, under Apple's privacy policy. In detail: the area is a square about 20 km wide centred on your iPhone's approximate position (the app requests about one-kilometre accuracy), together with the search words ("mosque", "mosquée", "مسجد"). Apple also provides the map shown on that screen. This happens only while the screen is open. If you tap Directions, Apple Maps opens with your current location and the mosque you chose. Nothing from this search is sent to ANA Finance, and the app does not keep the results after you leave the screen. Every other feature works without it; if you prefer, do not open Nearby mosques.

Compass

For the qibla, the app reads the iPhone's compass sensor on the device while the Qibla screen is open. Nothing is recorded or sent.

Notifications

Prayer notifications are local notifications scheduled by the app on your iPhone. There are no push notifications and no server is involved.

Stored on your iPhone

Your settings (city, calculation method, Asr school, adjustments, reminders, sounds, Hijri date offset), the last known location, the upcoming prayer muted with the bell, and the tasbih counter and goal. We cannot access them. Deleting the app deletes them. Like any app data, they may appear in your own iOS device backup (iCloud or computer), which you control.

Quran: not in the external beta. The Quran is not included in the external beta of Mueezin, so these two items do not exist there. In versions that include it (for example the internal beta), the app also keeps on your iPhone your last reading position and the text size. Like the rest, these two values never leave the device and are removed with the app.

What the app does not include

No account or sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting sent by the app, no third-party tracking SDK, no cross-app or cross-site tracking (the app does not request iOS tracking permission), no in-app purchases. The only external component in the app code is a prayer-time calculation library (Adhan, MIT licence) that runs on the device and does not use the network. The Nearby mosques search uses Apple's own MapKit framework, described above.

Beta version and TestFlight

During the beta, the app is distributed through TestFlight, an Apple service. Depending on your settings and what you choose to send, TestFlight may give ANA Finance: install date, number of sessions and crashes; your name and email address if you were invited by email; and the feedback you send with "Send Beta Feedback" (text, optional screenshot, device model and iOS version, and your email address if you provide it). This data comes from TestFlight, not from the app, and is governed by Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy. We use it only to fix the app.

Children

The app collects no data, including about children.

Your rights

The app sends us no personal data, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side in the app. For data received through TestFlight (feedback, email address), write to us and we will delete it on request.

Changes

If the app or this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
