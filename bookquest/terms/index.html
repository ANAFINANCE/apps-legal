BookQuest — Terms of Use

Effective 31 October 2026 · Published by ANA Finance, Dubai (United Arab Emirates)

BookQuest is free. You import your own PDFs, the app makes quizzes from them on your iPhone, and your books and progress stay on your device. Questions are made by AI and can contain mistakes: use them as a study aid, not as an authority.

1. About these terms

These terms describe how you may use BookQuest, an iPhone app published by ANA Finance, Dubai. By installing or using the app, you accept them. If you do not accept them, do not use the app. How the app handles data is described in the Privacy Policy.

2. What BookQuest does

You choose a PDF with the iOS file picker. The app reads its text on your iPhone, detects chapters, and turns each chapter into an island with multiple-choice questions. You play through a map with stars, bosses, XP, a daily streak and badges.

  • BookQuest is free. It has no in-app purchases, no ads and no account.
  • It works with PDFs that contain readable text. Password-protected PDFs and scanned PDFs without text cannot be used: the app tells you so and does not invent content.
  • Pages that cannot be read well, and pages such as copyright notices, tables of contents or acknowledgements, are left out of the quizzes.

3. Your PDFs and your content

  • Use only PDFs that you are allowed to use. You are responsible for having the right to use the documents you import.
  • Your PDF stays on your iPhone. The app keeps only the text it extracts, the chapters, the questions and your progress, in its own storage on your device. ANA Finance does not receive them. See the Privacy Policy for the two optional exceptions (downloading the Gemma models, and server mode).
  • You can delete a book in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone.

4. Questions made by AI

  • Questions are created by an AI model: by default Apple's on-device model, or the Gemma model once you have downloaded it, or a server you chose (section 6).
  • The app applies automatic checks before it keeps a question. For example, each question must be backed by a quote found on the page it cites, and duplicate questions, questions about the structure of the book (chapters, pages, table of contents) and questions with unclear choices are rejected. These checks reduce errors but do not remove them.
  • A question or an answer can still be wrong, unclear or incomplete. After you answer, the app offers the quote and the passage in an expandable box so you can check them against your book. Do not rely on BookQuest alone for exams, professional, medical, legal or financial decisions.

5. Progress, stars and badges

XP, stars, the daily streak and badges are part of the game. They are stored only on your iPhone, are not shared with other people, and have no value outside the app. They can be lost if you delete the app or your device data.

6. Optional features that use the network

  • Gemma models. On devices that can run them, the app offers once to download the Gemma models from the public Hugging Face repository (huggingface.co). This is optional, and you can delete the models in Settings. The models are provided by their publishers and the download service by Hugging Face, each under their own terms. BookQuest does not control them.
  • Server mode. It is off by default and only works if you type the https address of a server in Settings. If you do, the passages of your book used to create questions are sent to that server. Choose a server you trust: its operator, not ANA Finance, is responsible for what it does with them.

7. Third-party components

BookQuest uses Google's LiteRT-LM library (Apache-2.0) to run the Gemma model on your iPhone, and relies on the Apple system frameworks of your device. These components remain under their own licences. The Gemma model from Google is provided under the Gemma Terms of Use and its Prohibited Use Policy.

8. Availability and changes to the app

BookQuest is provided as it is. The app may change, and features may be added, changed or removed, including the optional features in section 6. New versions of the app are distributed through Apple's App Store or TestFlight, under Apple's rules.
